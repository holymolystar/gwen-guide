How to play Gwen

Poke with stacked Qs, survive the lanes that beat you early, and out-scale them into teamfights.

01Trading pattern

  • Stack Q first

    Auto the wave to 4 stacks before you walk up to trade.

  • Q, then E out

    Your basic short trade: leave before they can hit back.

  • Aim the center

    The middle of your Q deals true damage.

  • W blocks return damage

    Abilities from outside the mist can't target you.

  • Trade on their cooldowns

    When their key spell is down, it's a free trade.

02Power spikes

  • Level 6

    R gives you real all-in pressure.

  • First item

    Your biggest spike. Many hard lanes turn here.

  • Two items

    You win most side lanes.

  • Skill points

    Into lanes where you need to dodge, put 3 points in Q, then max E for a shorter dash cooldown.

03Lane and map

  • Let hard lanes push to you

    Farm near your tower and give up CS rather than die; XP matters most.

  • Cheater recall

    In lanes you win, push 3–4 waves into their tower and reset.

  • Don't leash

    When you need level 1 priority, like into Irelia or Singed.

  • Ping roams

    Riven, Pantheon and Kled love to leave lane.

04Teamfight role

  • Look for 5v5s

    Into late-game duelists like Fiora, Tryndamere, Yasuo and Yone.

  • Mist the backline out

    Use W in the fight so their ranged damage can't target you.

  • Tanks are fair game

    Your passive hits harder the more health they have.

  • Save R3

    Until they've used their dash or ultimate.
